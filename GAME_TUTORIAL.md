# Game Tutorial

| Input         | Action                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `Mouse`       | Move the platform left / right              |
| `Left Click`  | Launch the ball / interact with UI buttons  |

### Gameplay

- Move the **Mouse** left or right to control the platform.
- At the beginning of each level, press **Left Click** to launch the ball.
- The ball automatically bounces around the level and breaks the blocks it hits.
- Move the platform to follow the ball and make it bounce back into the play area.
- If the ball falls below the play area, **Game Over** is triggered.
- Break all blocks to complete the current level and continue to the next one.
- After completing the final level, the **Victory** screen is displayed.
- The mouse cursor is hidden during gameplay and becomes visible in the **Main Menu**, **Game Over**, and **Victory** screens.
- On UI screens, move the cursor over a button and press **Left Click** to activate it.
